# Session momentum USD-M: frozen protocol, 11 November 2026

This is a single historical experiment, not a prospective or independent test. The reused 2024–2026 sample is development history. Primary source: Shen, Urquhart and Wang, *Bitcoin intraday time-series momentum*, https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/100181/3/21Sep2021Bitcoin%20Intraday%20Time-Series%20Momentum.R2.pdf, equations (1)–(5), (8), table 8. The authors' exchange-specific Bitstamp session is 09:00–17:00 EST, chosen using its historical volume spike and CME break; no new Binance volume-optimized session is estimated. The paper says EST, without a verified DST conversion procedure. We interpret this literally as fixed UTC−05:00 year round, disclose this ambiguity, and use the same clock for BTC and ETH. This is an adaptation to Binance USD-M and ETH, not a reproduction of the paper's spot returns.

On each calendar day, let Cprev be the previous 17:00 session-end trade price, O30 the price at 09:30, C60 at 16:00, C30 at 16:30, C at 17:00, in that fixed offset. ONFH=O30/Cprev−1; SLH=C30/C60−1; LH=C/C30−1. First compute regressions LH on ONFH and on ONFH+SLH with intercept, sample size and R², separately by asset and segment; these are ex-post diagnostic associations, not trade returns. Article table 8 defines break-even as trading costs reducing its performance to zero; the table also reports 0.096% average per trading day for its combined rule. Do not conflate that number or its 10 bps with our fills.

Equation (8): long if ONFH>0 and SLH<0; short if ONFH<=0 and SLH>=0; otherwise cash. No volume, volatility, trend, discretionary stop, maker, leverage or additional strategy filters. Price at each time is the last closed 1m trade candle ending at that boundary. Signal becomes available one second after 16:30; modeled entry is the 16:32 1m open (strictly later, modeling publication plus delay); exit is 17:01 1m open. Stress entry is 16:35 and exit 17:05. Candle opens model execution, not evidence they were executable. No pyramiding, one position per asset/account, forced session-end exit. Closed-session dates define segments, and every initiated position is settled after its scheduled exit even if a reporting boundary intervenes; no cost or return is discarded.

Inputs: previous pinned Binance USD-M perpetual 1m trade and mark monthly archives and funding manifest `research/usdm_1m_manifest.json`, `research/usdm_5m_manifest.json`; check every downloaded byte against saved SHA-256, never silently substitute a revision. Warm-up December 2024, main 2025-01-01 to 2026-10-01 UTC (exclusive). Development is calendar 2025; check is 2026-01-01 to 2026-10-01, both already used elsewhere. Report 2025 H1, H2, 2026 H1 and Q3. Reject incomplete or conflicting required data, preserve provenance; no interpolation. Public archive timestamps are not actual historical reception times; modeled availability is disclosed, not observed latency.

Two separate starting cash accounts 1000 USDT each; sum only as separate accounts. At 1x use minimum quantity meeting the fixed, previously observed current BTC minNotional 50 / ETH minNotional 20 USDT, market step 0.001; these are current-proxy filters, not verified historic filters. Round upward, check notional <=100 and cash, using mark for notional and modeled trade price for cap, otherwise reject. Record every rejection. Historical filter changes and partial fills are not modeled. No account can borrow. Estimated loss gate uses the largest absolute 30-minute close-to-close move over the preceding 7 days, scaled by notional, plus two-sided stress friction/fees and 21 bps funding allowance; skip without complete warmup or if >5 USDT. This backward-looking scenario estimate is not a maximum loss or stop: a gap can exceed 5, especially in a short.

Base taker fee 5 bps per side of actual filled notional, half-spread 0.5 bps and additional impact 1.5 bps per side, total 7 bps per side before funding. Prior 30-minute BTC snapshot showed roughly 0.013 bps static round-trip spread, but no depth/time guarantee; two bps each side is an explicit conservative, not measured historical, friction assumption. Stress fee 5 bps plus 5 bps friction per side; delay as above. The saved engine applies adverse price impact once: gross at reference opens, friction = absolute execution-price adjustment, fee at adjusted price; do not charge spread a second time. Funding is the signed actual rate times mark notional only for funding timestamps strictly after entry and no later than exit; missing necessary rate/mark blocks the affected trade. Fee is an assumed public VIP0 taker level, not the owner's verified rate or historical fee schedule. Full fills assumed; maker fills, latency, spreads and historic filters are unverified. No automatic API calls or live orders.

Acceptance must meet all: >=100 completed trades per asset overall and >=30 per asset during 2026 check, >=90% valid session coverage in each asset/segment; positive net after both base and stress for each asset overall and each asset check; positive stress net in at least three of four segments per asset; positive aggregate stress net after removing the single best trade and after removing the better asset; closed-equity maximum drawdown <=2% of each 1000 USDT account in each scenario; >=20 trades/year/asset. These modest thresholds ensure multiple periods/assets and usable observation frequency, not statistical independence or proof of edge. Four segment comparisons and two assets imply selection/multiple-testing risk; no parameter alteration after results. Any unverified historical filter, quote, fill or source conflict limits operational readiness, irrespective of measured PnL. Report gross, fee, friction, signed funding, net; side/asset/segment; average, drawdown, holding, rejects, without-best-trade and without-best-asset; break-even full round-trip bps = 10000*(sum gross + signed funding)/sum entry reference notionals, not annual account return. Compare actual stress by recalculating its path, including size/risk gate.
