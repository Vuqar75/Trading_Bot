Scope: replace REST quote dependency of the existing paper Poller only. No new service, collector, financial writer, model call, strategy/risk change or order transport. Existing AI analyst continues its saved-budget scheduled REST capture; its availability does not drive local protection.

Both network threads only update bounded memory. The existing main process owns the ledger. The market socket demultiplexes verified combined envelopes; wrong symbols/type including COIN-M st=2 are rejected. No keys or private/Testnet endpoints.

The existing32768 trade queue/batch256 and continuity/reconnect latch remain. Final source choice is the official partial-depth top5 snapshot stream at100ms, not diff-depth reconstruction. Bid/ask are the first validated, strictly sorted positive-quantity levels; the full small raw snapshot and normalized BBO are retained. U/u/pu are checked; a broken pu chain is recorded and gates that cycle, then a fresh absolute snapshot can recover without reconstructing lost history. This does not prove intrainterval SL/TP visibility or maker fills. New quote memory: one latest absolute BBO, one latest mark, at most4096 protective component events; overflow clears the bounded event history and latches one unconfirmed cycle; independent latest absolute components are retained. Recovery uses only fresh current snapshots, never historical fills. This is top-of-book absolute data, not diff-depth reconstruction or maker-fill proof. Book u need not increment by one. Repeats do not refresh receipt or source age; revised same identity is rejected until a newer identity. Older identities are ignored without retroactive execution. Connection loss clears its component; recovery requires a new absolute component on that connection. Trade-history faults remain independent of fresh quote-based protection and require the existing safe trade recovery; no missing trade executions are replayed.

Every component independently checks source and event timestamps against the unchanged5000ms bound, future time, and receipt ordering. BBO freshness uses T AND E; mark freshness uses E. Composite execution time=min(component source times), received=max(component receipt times); local receipt never replaces source time. Both channels/components must be confirmed for any protective fill. Missing/stale/disconnected/revised/overflowed quote evidence blocks execution even if a previously cached quote is younger than5s. Public status includes quote_feeds separately from trade_feeds.

Fresh independent component events can latch SL (mark or exit-side BBO), only if their source is at/after opening and still fresh at actual observation. This does not fill: incomplete full quotes leave EXIT_PENDING. TIME remains a clock condition and is retained even without any market. The writer saves first detection (wall + monotonic), source component evidence, wait/recovery, and actual exit quote/fill separately, atomically with existing state. Protection is checked before bounded trade-batch processing. No future/result price, synthetic price, late execution or loosened limit.

For the final partial-depth source, exit liquidity consumption is keyed by symbol/exit side/visible price/quantity, persistently in the existing ledger metadata. A new u, deeper-level change, opposite-side update, mark update or reconnect cannot replenish the identical visible exit-side state. Identical states revisited later are conservatively not replenished; this may underfill and is not a queue/fill model. Legacy bookTicker fixtures retain their original u/T identity. A new composite quote with the same BBO cannot reuse already consumed visible quantity. Partial exits retain pending state across restart; new admissible BBO liquidity may close the remainder. Existing fees/slippage/funding/reconciliation and idempotent ledger handlers are reused. No guarantee of fills during complete market loss, partial liquidity, source delays or process failure.
