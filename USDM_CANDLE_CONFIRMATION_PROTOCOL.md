# USD-M 15m closed candle observation protocol

BTCUSDT USD-M trade klines (`/fapi/v1/klines`), UTC 15m, ms; public REST, no credential and no order. For each of four subsequent boundaries record the server time and exact one-candle raw response at approximately +2, +12, +30 and +60 seconds, and also re-read the previous observed candle after the following boundary. Stop after four boundaries or 65 minutes, maximum 2 MiB; timeout/errors are explicit and no missing observation is fabricated. The first boundary may precede the production change and is measurement only; count three later boundaries toward acceptance. Do not treat REST close timestamp or two equal observations as a guarantee of finality.

For live strategy inputs, preserve raw versions and signal publication times; mark a closed candle as preliminary, confirmation pending, accepted after a limited maturity period and repeated matching observations, contradictory on later change, or missing/unavailable. A changed preliminary version must not mutate a prior decision. Confirmation after the existing execution window does not authorize a backdated fill. An old conflict outside all signal inputs may remain archived and not gate future input; conflicts in the input window remain blocking. No strategy or budget changes.

Aggregate-trade sampling was `limit=20` recent snapshots, no `fromId`; three bounded `fromId` checks recovered 17, 47 and 1 IDs from the first three intervals. This demonstrates sampling omissions for those checks only, not a Binance outage or complete recovery of all 86 intervals.
