# Frozen hourly forecast experiment — 11 November 2026

Research adaptation of [Machine Learning-Based Bitcoin Trading Under Transaction Costs: Evidence From Walk-Forward Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00060v1). That study uses sequential training/validation/test folds and costs proportional to turnover, and finds naive sign trading deteriorates under costs. This is not a reproduction: two assets, 6-month/1-month fixed windows, two specified models, 60-second entry latency, 2% stop and different costs, without validation tuning. Data 2025–2026 were already used by the project; retrospective sequential results are not independent validation.

## Features at UTC hourly boundary T

Use only complete closed USD-M trade-price OHLCV minutes for the completed hour [T−1h,T). Denote C[T] its closing price, H[T], L[T], V[T] its high, low, summed volume. Exactly 12 features in this order:

1. Five simple returns `C[T]/C[T-k hours]-1` for k=1,2,4,12,24.
2. Sample standard deviation (ddof=1) of the last 24 and last 168 completed 1h simple close-to-close returns, including the return ending at T.
3. `(H[T]-L[T])/C[T]`.
4. `V[T]/median(V[T-168h],...,V[T-1h])-1`; median contains 168 preceding complete hourly volumes, excludes the current hour. Median <=0 is invalid; zero current volume with positive median is permitted and yields −1.
5. `C[T]/mean(C[T-23h],...,C[T])-1`, SMA24 including current close.
6. `sin(2*pi*UTC_hour(T)/24)` and `cos(...)`.

All closes must be strictly positive; complete sequential hours are mandatory for every component. Missing minute, missing prior hour, non-finite inputs or zero denominator invalidates that row; no forward filling. Boundary time T is model availability T+1 second (assumed, not historical observed receipt). Calendar time encoding uses the hour number at T, irrespective of DST. Input warm-up starts 2024-12-01. Training rows require a fully observed target; a test prediction can be saved without its later target, which must be labeled unavailable rather than dropped retrospectively.

## Target, windows and models

Target `open_1m(T+61min)/open_1m(T+1min)-1`, a raw simple trade-price return, with both opens from the exact named UTC minutes. The entry and target opens are model references, not proven executable quotes; no stop, fees or funding enter the regression target. These labels must be completely observed strictly **before** a training window's next test-month start. Every test month uses six preceding calendar months of eligible hourly boundary rows, `[month_start - 6 calendar months, month_start)`, with label exit `< month_start`. No random split, monthly retraining only; predictions made once per hour, models fixed during month. First train January–June 2025, tests July 2025–October 2026 inclusive: 16 monthly folds per asset, maximum 32 XGBoost fits and 32 Ridge fits total. Earlier test observations become permissible training rows only after their target has closed before the new boundary.

One preselected XGBoost regressor: n_estimators=200, max_depth=3, learning_rate=0.03, min_child_weight=20, subsample=1, colsample_bytree=1, reg_lambda=10, objective=reg:squarederror, random_state=42, n_jobs=2. One control Ridge alpha=10, standardization mean and population SD fitted on each training set only; zero-variance training feature uses scale=1. XGBoost uses raw features. No parameter or feature search, no early stopping, no selection by test performance. XGBoost is the only possible candidate; Ridge and cash are controls.

## Decision, execution and risk

For both cost scenarios, threshold `abs(pred)>=0.00375` (37.5 bps, 1.25 × 30 bps stress round-trip before funding), LONG positive, SHORT negative; no entry below threshold. Ridge follows the identical threshold. Diagnostic XGBoost sign-only uses identical saved predictions, no refit, and is not a candidate. At most one position per separate 1000-USDT asset/model/scenario account; signals while open/pending ignored and recorded. Fee 5 bps per side on filled notional; base impact including spread 5 bps per side, stress impact 10 bps per side. Base entry is minute open at T+1m, stress at T+6m; exit 60 minutes after actual entry unless 2% stop from adjusted entry. Gap-stop uses adverse bar open or stop level and impact, with 1m OHLC ambiguity resolved pessimistically; liquidation/stop ordering conservative, model execution not guaranteed. Funding actual slot/time and rate, 1m mark-open proxy, uncertain stop/funding order conservatively debited and flagged. Hold and stop can make executed return differ from target.

Use the existing 1m trade, mark and funding manifests on D:, including separately pinned October 2026 revised versions documented in EXTREME_REVERSAL_PROTOCOL.md. No archive versions silently changed, no wholesale redownload. Current metadata proxy: BTC min notional 50, ETH 20 USDT, step/min qty .001; q rounded down so mark and adjusted trade entry notionals <=100, 1x, cash incl fees, model stop-risk and stress/funding reserve <=5 USDT; reject if cannot satisfy. Filters are current proxy, not historical truth. No borrowing, capital reset or working-account transfer. At end of data, positions left open are reported as unresolved with last valid mark, never fabricated as realized closure, and invalidate admission.

## Gates and reporting, fixed before results

XGBoost with threshold qualifies for prospective research only if all: >=30 completed per asset with >=10 LONG and >=10 SHORT each; each asset net positive base and stress; combined stress net positive in >8 of 16 sequential test months; combined stress net excluding the single best completed trade >0; conservative mark-to-market drawdown of each asset account <=2% in both scenarios; no unresolved position/data or financial integrity issues. Insufficient counts are a separate outcome, never lower threshold. Model complexity is not justified if XGBoost advantage over Ridge is not established: compare same periods and assets after costs, note that an exploratory point comparison is not statistical proof, and do not declare the control the new candidate. Cash baseline net zero.

Keep all hourly predictions with model/asset/fold/training window, T, target exit and outcome or missing target; filtered decisions, signal ignores and execution/financial ledger by asset, direction, scenario and monthly exit; gross, fee, impact, funding, realized net, full account MTM, holds, stop causes, eligible-prediction average versus actual return, forecast-error metrics, accepted threshold counts, costs versus gross, and break-even round-trip bps defined as `10000*(gross+signed funding−liquidation_fee)/sum(entry_reference_notional)` on completed trades. Each account continues across month boundaries without reseeding; assign full trade net to exit month. Entry at the very end that cannot be exited with available data is explicitly unresolved, not an invented exit. Save input hashes, dependency versions and reproducible journal/summary.
