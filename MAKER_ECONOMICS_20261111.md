## Source, coverage and calculation

Inputs are the previously committed `research/usdm_screen_summary.csv.gz` (SHA-256 `1be8d70ac22aa62f3155854eee677fe9862ee719188a64b7d24dbc4ad8ea21b5`) and session journal `research/session_momentum_results/journal.json.gz` (SHA-256 `1a8620b4d6e6e659901c94f8f5d78f648c681d4800056a4b467ed4fbfd9486af`). No market data, new trades, or new backtests are used. `python -B -m research.maker_economics` verifies each old segment's gross + signed funding − fees − impact = net, and writes **all 359 gross-positive rows** to `research/maker_economics.csv`; its SHA-256 is `1dc6f073f61f35e04c5976e6e50ddf35362691622af1500bd72440461692356c`. This file is the complete considered positive-gross selection, not only the examples below. The 48 `spot_fee_no_funding` attribution rows are excluded because they intentionally disable funding and duplicate paths. Stress and base are different execution paths and scenarios, not independent observations.

For the original intraday universe, each scenario has 414 asset/side/configuration groups: base has 173 with gross >0, of which 89 exceed maker/maker and 69 exceed approximate maker/taker fees; stress has 177, of which 95 and 70 respectively exceed them. Session-momentum adds six gross-positive base groups, one over each fee threshold, and three stress groups, one over maker/maker and none over approximate maker/taker. LONG, SHORT and BOTH in the intraday screen are separate simulated paths; the session side rows partition the BOTH path. They and assets are not summed as independent evidence.

The old USD-M engine calculates gross from underlying reference price changes before fee and impact; funding is separate. Impact nevertheless changes the saved stop price and path. Some segment summaries contain hypothetical mark-to-market liquidation of a still-open position rather than a completed trade; `marked_open_segments` and `entries` alongside `closed_trades` disclose this. Sessions were reset to cash separately. The saved intraday turnover is both sides, including hypothetical marked exits. Let `D = turnover_both_sides / 2`; full round-trip break-even = `10000*(gross+signed funding)/D` bps. This D is a symmetric reference, not precisely the initial notional if entry and exit prices differ. Public regular USDT-M [Binance maker/taker schedule](https://www.binance.com/en-BH/fee/futureFee) is 2/5 bps per side without VIP, BNB discount, rebate or promotion; maker/maker is **exactly 4 bps on D**, while maker-entry/taker-exit is **approximately 7 bps** because the aggregate old data lack separate entry/exit notionals. It could vary with their ratio; the CSV's mixed-fee value is labeled approximate. Session rows also expose exact maker-entry/taker-exit fee at reference prices, because their individual entry and exit notionals are saved. All are hypothetical fees on the unchanged saved taker path. No spread is credited, no old impact is subtracted a second time, no maker fill is assumed. Account-specific and historical fees are unverified.

## Proposed single future mechanics protocol — not started

1. Original signal: on a closed 1h candle, SHORT only on a fresh cross below the lowest close of the preceding 48 hours; exit when close crosses above the highest close of the preceding 24 hours, stop 0.75% above actual entry, maximum holding 48h. No other strategy, parameter search or pyramiding. A signal consumes only candle versions already accepted at decision time.
2. Model decision at actual closed-candle receipt plus 1 second, then 200 ms simulated order latency. If data are stale, gapped or order book not synchronized, refuse entry. For a SHORT sell, a post-only order is placed at the best ask seen at arrival, tick-rounded, only if it cannot cross; otherwise reject/cancel. TTL 60 seconds, no chasing and no second entry for that signal. Once a partial fill occurs, cancel remainder at TTL and retain only confirmed simulated quantity; if below applicable minimum, label unexecutable rather than fabricate a full fill.
3. At exit signal use a post-only buy at the then-best bid, for remaining position, TTL 30 seconds; cancel and execute the remainder as taker after a further 200 ms using available depth. At stop or 48h expiry, cancel pending orders and model immediate taker close, not maker. Keep 0.75% protective rule attached to partial quantity, apply funding and fees per fill and compare mark and trade prices separately. On simultaneous or unsequenced events choose the adverse defensible ordering; unknown state is unresolved, not a successful exit. Latency stress uses +1 second both directions and repeats the fill path rather than applying a cosmetic fee discount.
4. Independently reconstruct USD-M BTCUSDT depth from a REST snapshot plus the documented WebSocket diff-depth `U/u/pu` sequence, record source and receive timestamps, all gaps, reset and resynchronize on any sequence loss. Save aggregate trades with source and receive times and sequence, book ticker, periodic snapshots, metadata, mark and funding. Public L2 depth and trades do not identify individual order ownership or exact queue position: model initial displayed queue ahead and report conservative and optimistic cancellation-allocation bounds, partial fills, order expiry, post-fill 1/5/30-second adverse selection and later protective exits. Never assert a fill solely from a price touch or a single REST snapshot.
5. Future data minimum: first 7 days for sequencing, clocks, coverage and storage audit, then at least 60 days and at least 20 fresh qualifying signals before an engineering readout; if 20 signals do not occur, extend rather than backfill. A prospective economics readout additionally requires at least 50 eligible signals across at least 180 days, predeclared 5 bps taker and 2 bps maker per side, full funding and no overlap with strategy selection. This is a minimum, not proof. Before any future collection, choose a disk quota from measured first-week rate, retention and fail-closed stop; no collector or storage quota is created now. Exchange L2 is insufficient for exact queue position even over a longer interval.
6. Predeclared rejection: fewer than 95% of relevant event intervals sequence-complete; clocks too uncertain to order signal/receipt/decision/fill; any unaccounted financial event; lower-bound model fill ratio below 30%; nonpositive net after fees, funding and worst defensible queue/adverse-selection model; or nonpositive stress result. Failure of any one rejects prospective admission. Require the risk-estimation and filter checks of the existing research account, 1x, one BTC position, <=100 USDT notional and <=5 USDT estimated risk; no real orders. Current 50-USDT minNotional and 0.001 step must be checked at each future decision, and a gap can exceed estimated risk. Sufficient sample and positive lower-bound net do not prove future profitability.

## Disk and integrity

The constrained filesystem is the Windows **C:** volume, also underlying the checkout and WSL virtual disks. At the start of this task C: had 337,895,424 bytes free; Ubuntu `df /` reports capacity inside its virtual disk, not guaranteed physical room on C:. The VM was not used to infer this figure. npm's checked cache index contained 1,136 entries, all on public `registry.npmjs.org`; its standard cache clean removed refetchable cache. `pip cache info` showed 282 HTTP cache files and zero locally built wheels; `pip cache purge` removed only that cache. Six temporary USD-M `.tar` files were deleted only after byte-for-byte SHA-256 comparison with their retained `.tar.gz` counterparts. The 89-file 82,945,674-byte reproducible session cache was copied to `D:/TradingBot-reproducible-cache-20261111/session-momentum-cache`, each copy hash-checked twice, then only the corresponding C: temporary copies were removed. Existing research manifests and compressed result journals remain in Git. To reproduce with this moved cache use `python -B -m research.session_momentum D:/TradingBot-reproducible-cache-20261111/session-momentum-cache research/session_momentum_results`; missing mark archives are fetched only after the pinned SHA check. Raw user files, working checkouts, databases, credentials, locks and backups were not removed.

The C: free-space figure after these actions was approximately 1.82 GB (decimal), still less than 1% of its 262 GB capacity and not a healthy long-term margin. Files in `DockerDesktopUpdates/in_progress` were deliberately retained: its two largest ISO files did **not** match the currently installed copies. Further space requires identifying additional nonessential user data or separately completing/clearing that update through its owner; do not delete the staged update merely based on its name. VM, trading services, all pause settings, AI/Donchian timers, budgets and credentials were not changed.
