# Frozen protocol: two-hour extreme reversal, 11 November 2026

## Origin, distinction and scope

Research adaptation of De Nicola, [On the Intraday Behavior of Bitcoin](https://ledgerjournal.org/ojs/ledger/article/download/213/212/1232), DOI 10.5195/ledger.2021.213. The article examines BTC/USD Bitstamp 2015–2018 and intraday return reversal; this study uses Binance BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT USD-M perpetual 2025–October 2026. Different market, period, volatility estimator, latency, 2% stop and cost assumptions mean no claim to reproduce the article's performance. Candle data cannot establish the cause of an extreme move or liquidation cascades. Prior fixed families A breakout, B trend MA crossing, C fixed-range return and D volume continuation do not implement the rule below; session momentum combines signs at clock times. No same configuration or journal has been identified.

UTC 2h bars, formed from complete consecutive 1m trade candles. r[t]=ln(close[t]/close[t-1]); sigma[t]=sample standard deviation (ddof=1) of r[t-360]..r[t-1], excluding current. At close, LONG if r[t]<=-3*sigma[t], SHORT if r[t]>=3*sigma[t], otherwise no signal. Missing input, gaps or sigma<=0 reject; all 360 prior returns require complete bars. Each signal records exact closes, r, sigma, input window and first model availability. Historical receipt time is unknown: assume boundary+1 second for signal availability, explicitly modeled.

One position per asset/account; 1000 USDT independently each, 1x and no borrowed cash, notional <=100 USDT. Signals while open or pending are recorded and ignored, not queued. Base entry is the 1m trade open 60 seconds after signal boundary; stress is 360 seconds after. These are modeled open prices and not demonstrated available fills. Position size floor to quantity step 0.001 using adverse modeled entry price, subject to <=100 entry notional, available cash including fee, BTC market minQty/step 0.001 and mark-based minNotional 50, ETH 0.001 and minNotional 20; current-public metadata is a proxy, not historically proven. If filter, mark, funds or any input cannot be verified, reject and record. At entry reserve calculated 2% adverse stop-distance plus two-sided scenario fees/impact and 21 bps funding allowance; reject if >5 USDT. Gaps can exceed estimated risk. No transfer, augmentation after losses or increased size to meet a minimum.

Stop at entry fill*(1-0.02) LONG or *(1+0.02) SHORT, fixed on entry. Check every 1m trade candle; stop and liquidation checked conservatively with mark extremes and stop-first ordering. When a trade bar crosses stop, sell/buy reference is the worse of bar open and stop (gap adverse), then apply exit impact; this does not guarantee market execution. Without stop, exit on 1m open exactly 120 minutes after modeled entry. Check time exit at that open before inspecting the rest of that minute; no target or pyramiding. Funding events at entry<=event<exit, using actual event rate and contemporaneous mark; sign negative for LONG and positive for SHORT on positive funding. If funding and a stop occur in the same 1m bar with uncertain order, include debit and exclude unconfirmed credit; flag ambiguity. No missing funding is replaced by zero. Base fee 5 bps each side on executed notional plus 5 bps adverse price impact each side; stress fee 5 and impact 10 bps/side, extra five minutes entry delay. Impact already includes spread; never charge spread again. Full fills assumed, no maker. USD-M trade price is execution reference, mark is risk/MTM only. Current metadata, historical spread, queue, real latency and liquidity during jumps are not established.

Segments by exit time UTC: 2025H1 [2025-01-01,2025-07-01), 2025H2 [2025-07-01,2026-01-01), 2026H1 [2026-01-01,2026-07-01), 2026H2 [2026-07-01,2026-11-01). Accounts never reset at segment boundaries; entry/exit and complete funding/fees of a trade are assigned to its exit segment for reporting, while actual account cash/equity is continuous. December 2024 is warmup only; no position before Jan 1. At 2026-11-01 a still-open position is NOT invented as closed: retain it, compute an explicitly unrealized mark using the final available minute's mark close and estimated exit fee/impact, show separately, and exclude it from closed-trade criteria. Its final mark and any missing/uncertain funding must be disclosed, never represented as a known realized exit.

Minute inputs from pinned official Binance USD-M trade, mark and funding manifests `research/usdm_1m_manifest.json` and `research/usdm_5m_manifest.json`, 2024-12 through 2026-10. Use D: for archives/cache and outputs; preserve original manifest and SHA-256 versions, receive timestamps as archive provenance rather than inventing historical availability. If pinned archives or pinned REST gap fills are unavailable, never substitute revisions: retain gap, refuse affected entries, stop or mark the open position unresolved if protection/funding/valuation cannot be verified, and do not claim a completed strategy result beyond an unresolved financial state. No fill at past candle open. Funding completeness must be checked against documented slots, not inferred as zero. Exact path/order of intrabar liquidation and stop remains uncertain and is modeled pessimistically; report such cases.

Decision gates fixed before results: >=30 closed each asset; >=10 LONG and >=10 SHORT each; positive full-period base net and stress net on each asset; positive combined independent-account stress realized net in >=3 of 4 exit segments; combined stress net excluding the single best completed trade >0; worst conservative intraminute mark-to-market drawdown of each account <=2% in both scenarios. Net of both accounts is the sum of two separate 1000-USDT accounts, not a joint position limit. Insufficient counts are distinct from negative PnL. Unknown or unresolved positions prohibit admission. All metrics, signal counts, refused entries, ignores, model fills, full cost/funding ledger, risk and timing, side/asset/period, worst trade gap, reason and no-best-trade will be published. Break-even bps denominator is sum entry reference notionals on completed trades, calculated as 10000*(gross+signed funding)/denominator; not account return. User's fixed parameters and gates are not changed after results. Data were used previously, so this is not independent evidence.
