# USD-M baseline continuation protocol

Preserve the experiment start, both financial histories and all previous data epochs. A single continuation may be created only while both isolated experiments are demonstrably flat, have no pending queue or route actions, have no reservations or pending transfers, and reconcile. Use the existing cycle lock and transaction guards. Before activation, independently receive exactly the existing feed's bounded BTCUSDT USD-M trade 15m closed window twice, at least ten seconds apart; verify source, UTC boundaries, continuity, raw parsing, maturity, equal hashes and freshness. Persist both actual reception times and raw versions, and explicitly mark original historical availability unknown. An accepted window may later be revised; any conflict in the current dependency window blocks entry. No tolerance or deletion is introduced.

The new epoch is a versioned continuation linked to the old epoch and does not reset accounts. Historical bars only warm prospective decisions: no signal whose close precedes the continuation may enter, and no historical price may be used as a future execution. Retry must return the same continuation or fail closed; it must never silently create another epoch. Preserve the existing 100-USDT notional, 1x, 5-USDT estimated-risk, funding, filters, liquidity, execution and single-exposure constraints. The prior baseline is a Spot 4H SMA20 regime classifier; futures trade candles provide SHORT strategy data, not its classifier input. Do not mislabel those sources. A new prospective baseline snapshot must be obtained after continuation; do not rebrand the previous 4H decision as newly observed. Paid AI remains disabled and an old AI decision cannot grant an entry.

Keep two independent virtual accounts, no financial transfer or reseeding. Report separately the baseline's source/time/regime and futures trend-window source/time/features, plus signal or explicit no-signal, admission and rejection reasons. Two successive real 15m windows are required; reuse of one unchanged 4H baseline regime is not two independent 4H classifier observations. No artificial trade.

For the continuation only, allow one newly timed deterministic baseline capture within the current 4H period. Save it under the new epoch and the existing 4H boundary, without replacing the previous epoch's capture. It uses the same fixed Spot 4H SMA20 rule and expiry; it does not create an AI response. All subsequent regime refreshes retain the existing 4H schedule. In both accounts reject a decision whose data or AI answer predates the continuation; protection of an already-open position, if any, is evaluated before that gate. The baseline may be reused until expiry, and individual 15m routing evaluations are not independent regime decisions.
