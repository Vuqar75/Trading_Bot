# Frozen published-EMA transfer protocol, 11 November 2026

Author study [Mroziewicz and Ślepaczuk](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.10785v1), code `tmr-crypto/wf_optim_crypto@8be3ecf0019e991962f6f59bf9938cfa56411350`, independent eight-path review in `PUBLISHED_EMA_REPLICATION_REVIEW.md`. This is a modern USD-M BTC/ETH adaptation, not a reproduction of author profitability, nor independent validation: 2025–2026 were already project development data. Do not rerun all historical author paths or do a new outer sweep.

Input 1m UTC trade/mark/funding archives on D:, source hashes and explicit October 2026 revision in `research/extreme_reversal_results/source_restore.json` and `EXTREME_REVERSAL_PROTOCOL.md`. Complete UTC hour [T-1h,T) is stamped by **close boundary T**; price is the last completed minute close, corresponding to author xts timestamp minute 59. Hour is invalid if any constituent minute is missing; no backward fill, and no new trade until indicators/history are valid. 2024-12 is warmup only; evaluate [2025-01-01, 2026-11-01) using UTC boundaries, independently for BTC and ETH. No lookahead. Public minute opens and archive close times are modeled reference prices, not demonstrated historic execution or receipt times.

EMA is TTR-style: SMA seed at period n−1, alpha=2/(n+1), then recursive, calculated once over the complete series, not reinitialized at walk-forward boundaries. Periods 5,7,10,15,20,30 fast and 40,50,100,150,200 slow; exactly 30 pairs, ordering slow-major then fast-minor, matching author script. At each close T the per-pair direction for **the subsequent hourly return** is +1 if fast EMA(T)>slow EMA(T), otherwise -1; equality is SHORT. The value already applied to the hourly return ending T is the direction calculated at T−1h. Require all 200h EMA warmup and full training history; no backfill. The author replay uses only completed test blocks; for modern transfer the terminal block is clipped to the final data boundary and explicitly marked, with no imaginary future prices.

Two fixed schedules: 14-day training / 10-day testing and 7-day training / 28-day testing, test starts 2025-01-01 00:00 UTC and advances by the respective testing span; final testing block clips at 2026-11-01. At each test boundary T, compute the author's continuous per-pair one-hour-lagged signed log returns on the preceding 14×24 or 7×24 hourly bars **ending no later than T**, `p[t]*ln(C[t]/C[t-1]) - 0.001*abs(p[t]-p[t-1])` (unknown initial transition treated as zero, as the original), based solely on history and **without this transfer's stop or funding**. Select maximum training Sharpe `sqrt(365*24)*mean / sample_sd(ddof=1)`, risk-free zero; undefined zero SD ignored, exact tie selects first pair in slow-major/fast-minor order; if all invalid remain flat and record. Keep same choices for base and stress. Record train start/end, last data close, test start/end, scores and selected pair. A small retained author selection example must be checked from authenticated saved artifacts; the historical eight paths are not recomputed.

Result A is separate author-style unit signed-log diagnostic of the stitched chosen positions: `p[t]*ln(C[t]/C[t-1])`, where p[t] comes from chosen pair and preceding completed hourly close. Charge actual stitched exposure `0.001*abs(p[t]-p[t-1])`, initially flat: initial entry costs one unit, reversal two, selection switch with unchanged exposure costs zero. Report final 0.001 terminal close separately and include it only in explicitly terminally closed diagnostic; this is **not** USDT-M settlement, execution, short PnL or admitted performance.

Result B reuses existing paper research financial/mark/funding arithmetic in an offline state machine (no service, no real endpoint): separate 1000-USDT asset/schedule/scenario accounts, 1x, one position, fixed quantity until closed, <=100 USDT notional with qty rounded down, BTC current-proxy min notional 50/step 0.001, ETH 20/0.001; available capital, minQty and mark price constraints checked. Risk budget 5 USDT estimated from 2% stop plus two-sided scenario costs and 21bps funding reserve; adverse gaps can exceed risk estimate. At each UTC hourly boundary after the close, selected EMA direction is the desired exposure. Signal goes to execution at T+60 seconds (base) or T+360 seconds (stress); fee=5bps/side, combined spread/impact=5bps/side base or 10bps/side stress, never charge spread again. Fresh public minute data are historical modeled prices, not proof of fills.

A selected-pair change **without a direction change** does not touch the open quantity or charge fees; a true direction reversal closes then opens at modeled price, with separate quantities/fees/impact on both sides. Capital and positions remain continuous across all optimization and calendar boundaries. Funding is actual recorded event time/rate on mark-price minute-open proxy; debit/credit ambiguity when a protective stop and funding occur inside the same minute is conservatively debited, not invented credit. A 2% stop from adjusted entry is checked every minute, mark-based liquidation first, then adverse gap-stop (`min(open,stop)` LONG / `max` SHORT) and exit impact. After a stop, remain flat until a **strictly later completed hourly signal boundary**; cancel any pending action based on a signal before or simultaneous with that stop. At the next later boundary, a continuing same direction may re-enter. No additional time exit or target, no averaging, no automatic restart. Missing price/mark/funding blocks new entries; if an open position cannot be protected/valued, keep it explicitly unresolved and invalidate admission, never invent a stop/fill. At data end retain an open position, report unrealized mark less estimated exit costs separately, and do not call it closed.

All fees charged on notional, impact from adjusted fills, gross reference-price move, signed funding separately; funding rates not zero-filled. Capture every signal, pair choice, hold/reversal/stop, rejected or pending intent, order-like modeled fills, cost components, realized/unrealized and position state. Entries and capital never reset at test or four reporting segments (2025H1, 2025H2, 2026H1, 2026H2 through Oct); full completed trade PnL assigned to exit period. Same-size buy/hold is one LONG entered at first model-eligible BTC/ETH timestamp, same sizing/cost/funding/mark rules, no strategy signals or stop, mark at end, never presented as working account. Cash=0 trading PnL. Compute trades LONG/SHORT, gross/fee/impact/funding/net, max conservative mark-to-market drawdown, median/p90/max holding and fraction <=24h.

Precommitted gates **for each schedule**: >=30 completed trades on each asset, with >=10 LONG and >=10 SHORT each; base and stress net positive for both BTC and ETH; combined independent-account stress net positive in >=3 of four calendar exit segments; combined stress net without its best trade positive; max MTM drawdown <=2% on each account for both scenarios. Additionally, unless >=90% of completed trades close within 24h, it is not a short-term candidate for the owner's use. Insufficient samples and negative economics are separate findings; never choose the winner based solely on test results, never change settings or expand the search. Account for cost, exposure continuity, final residual, filters, funding, signal availability and execution uncertainty in the final decision. Any candidate remains uninstalled and disabled.
